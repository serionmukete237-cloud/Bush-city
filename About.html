Who We Are

Building Opportunities Through Property Ownership

Bush City Holdings is a property and land investment company based in Buea, Cameroon. We are dedicated to helping individuals and families discover and acquire land in carefully selected locations across the Buea area.

Our approach is built on transparency, client guidance, and a commitment to making the property acquisition process accessible and understandable. We believe that land ownership is one of the most meaningful investments a person can make.

Whether you are looking for a residential plot to build your family home, or exploring land as a long-term investment, we are here to guide you through the process with clarity and professionalism.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our Vision
